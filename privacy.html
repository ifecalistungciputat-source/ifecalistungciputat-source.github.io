Privacy Policy – Calistung Ciputat Konten

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Calistung Ciputat Konten ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by Calistung Ciputat (Ciputat, Indonesia). It is used only by Calistung Ciputat to publish its own educational videos to its own TikTok account (@calistungciputat).

1. Information we access

When the account owner connects a TikTok account, the App receives, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • an access token and refresh token, used only to upload and publish videos that the account owner has chosen.

The App does not access followers, messages, comments, analytics or any data of other TikTok users.

2. How we use information

Information is used only to publish videos selected by the account owner from the owner's own storage to the owner's own TikTok account. We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer inside a self-hosted automation tool, protected by the owner's login. Video files stay in the owner's own Google Drive.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the TikTok account is connected. The owner can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and websites), after which the tokens stop working and are deleted.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect information from children. It is operated by adults managing an educational account.
